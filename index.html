PayPal AP Policy Gate

An agent that cannot pay outside its policy, and proves every payment afterwards.

For accounts-payable teams at small and mid-size firms that let an AI agent prepare or make supplier payments through PayPal.

The problem

An AI model that reads supplier invoices can be told what to do by the invoice. One added line can swap the account that gets paid.

So the model never holds the money. It proposes a payment. A fixed set of rules, the gate, holds the PayPal credentials and decides.

  1. InvoiceText from a supplier
  2. Model proposesNo PayPal credentials
  3. Gate decidesHolds the credentials
  4. PayPalOnly if the gate says pay

Try it yourself

Paste a supplier invoice of your own. Two ways to run it.

  • In this browser

    This page has no AI model. A simple field reader stands in for it. The real gate code decides. Nothing is sent anywhere and nothing is paid.

    Try your own invoice

  • On your own PayPal Sandbox

    Run the desk on your machine. Paste a supplier invoice. The live model proposes, the gate decides, PayPal pays. Approve on a held payment pays it. Node.js 20 or newer.

    node gate/desk-server.mjs
node gate/desk-server.mjs --simulate

    The first needs your Sandbox keys in the environment. The second needs no keys and makes no PayPal call.

What is not built

  • The hosted pages make no PayPal call. They replay recorded runs and run the gate in your browser.
  • On the local desk the approver is a typed name plus a session token, not a login.
  • The purchase-order register is a sample. Nothing reads real documents yet.

All the known limits are in the README.
